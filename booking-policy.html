Booking, Payment & Cancellation Policy

Clear, truthful information regarding how hotel room enquiries, reservations, check-in, payments, and cancellations operate at Lee Comfort Kushalnagar.

Property: Lee Comfort Kushalnagar Location: Opposite RMC, SH 88, Kushalnagar Primary Contact: +91 95919 04010

1. Room Enquiry vs. Confirmed Reservation

Submitting a booking enquiry form on our website or starting a WhatsApp chat does not constitute a confirmed room reservation.

The website enquiry form serves solely to initiate direct communication with hotel staff. A reservation is only confirmed once hotel management verifies room availability for your specific travel dates and sends you an explicit written confirmation along with total pricing details.

2. Availability Confirmation & Room Pricing

Room rates at Lee Comfort Kushalnagar vary based on seasonality, travel dates, weekends/holidays, and guest occupancy.

  • Before any payment is collected, hotel management will provide a clear written total price including applicable taxes and fees.
  • We do not quote hidden resort fees, booking surcharges, or undisclosed mandatory charges.
  • Any extra bed or extra guest accommodation must be requested and approved in advance.

3. Advance Payment & Booking Guarantee

Depending on seasonal demand (e.g., peak holiday periods, long weekends), the hotel may request an advance token deposit to hold a room reservation.

Any advance payment instructions will be communicated directly from our verified hotel number (+91 95919 04010). The remaining room balance is payable upon physical check-in at the hotel front desk.

4. Accepted Payment Methods

Accepted methods for confirmed reservations and on-site payments include:

  • Unified Payments Interface (UPI)
  • Cash at check-in
  • Direct bank transfer (as provided by hotel management)

Note: This website does not collect card payments or bank login details online.

5. Cancellation, Amendment & Refund Terms

To maintain transparency and avoid misleading claims:

  • Cancellation Notice: Guests wishing to cancel or modify a confirmed reservation should notify hotel management via phone or WhatsApp as early as possible before the scheduled arrival date.
  • Refund Conditions: Where an advance deposit was paid, refund eligibility and applicable cancellation deductions depend on the advance notice period provided and will be confirmed in writing by hotel management at the time of booking.
  • No-Show Policy: Guests who do not arrive on the scheduled check-in date without prior written notice may forfeit their reservation hold and any advance deposit.

6. Check-in & Check-out Timings

Our standard operational timings (subject to final operator confirmation) are:

  • Standard Check-in Time: 12:00 PM (Noon)
  • Standard Check-out Time: 11:00 AM
  • Early Check-in / Late Check-out: Subject to room availability and prior arrangement with hotel management.

7. Mandatory Government Identification (Indian Law)

In compliance with local government regulations and police notification requirements:

  • All adult guests (18 years and above) must present a valid original Government-issued photo identification proof upon check-in.
  • Acceptable documents for Indian citizens include Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving Licence. PAN cards are not accepted as address proof.
  • Foreign nationals must present a valid original Passport and valid Indian Visa.

8. Guest Conduct & Property Rules

To preserve a clean, peaceful environment for all travelers:

  • Lee Comfort Kushalnagar is a lodging hotel property for registered guests.
  • Unregistered visitors are not permitted in guest rooms after 10:00 PM.
  • Illegal substances and hazardous items are strictly prohibited on property premises.
  • Guests are responsible for maintaining reasonable care of room furnishings and fittings.
