Coorg Attractions Near Kushalnagar

Lee Comfort Kushalnagar offers a convenient town base on State Highway 88 for travelers exploring the major sightseeing destinations across Kushalnagar and the Coorg region.

Tourist Information Disclosure: The attractions listed below are independent public and regional destinations. Lee Comfort Kushalnagar is a dedicated hotel accommodation provider and does not own, operate, manage, or sell entry tickets for these locations. Distances and driving times are approximate.

Golden Temple (Namdroling)

Approx. 5 - 6 km from hotel

Namdroling Monastery, widely known as the Golden Temple, is a major Tibetan Buddhist center featuring gold-plated statues, ornate prayer halls, and peaceful monastery grounds in Bylakuppe.

Cauvery Nisargadhama

Approx. 3 km from hotel

A natural river island formed by the River Cauvery, known for its lush bamboo groves, hanging bridge, deer park, and walking trails popular with families.

Dubare Elephant Camp

Approx. 14 km from hotel

A well-known sanctuary situated on the banks of the River Cauvery where visitors can observe elephants in a natural river setting, with seasonal boating.

Harangi Dam & Reservoir

Approx. 9 km from hotel

A masonry dam built across the Harangi tributary, offering scenic reservoir water views and a peaceful garden setting.

Kushalnagar Town & Market

Within walking distance

Lee Comfort is located right on SH 88 in Kushalnagar town, giving guests immediate access to restaurants, coffee shops, medical stores, ATMs, and local bus stands.

Madikeri Day Trip

Approx. 30 km uphill via SH 88

Kushalnagar is an ideal gateway for a day trip to Madikeri, including Raja's Seat viewpoint, Madikeri Fort, and Abbey Falls, before returning to your comfortable town hotel.
