Our Property

Dedicated Hotel Accommodation in Kushalnagar

Lee Comfort Kushalnagar is an independent hotel established to provide straightforward, clean, and reliable accommodation to travelers visiting Kushalnagar and the surrounding Coorg region.

Located directly on State Highway 88 (SH 88), opposite the Regulated Market Committee (RMC) yard and adjacent to the Woodland Showroom, our hotel offers excellent road connectivity for tourists, business visitors, and transit travelers.

We focus exclusively on providing hotel lodging across two core room categories: AC Deluxe Rooms and Standard Non-AC Rooms. Every room features an attached private bathroom with hot water supply, comfortable bedding, and WiFi access.

Exclusivity of Service Notice: Lee Comfort Kushalnagar is dedicated exclusively to operating this single hotel property in Kushalnagar. We do not operate third-party homestays, swimming pools, outdoor adventure camps, or resort villas.
