3. How Enquiry Information Is Processed (Client-Side WhatsApp)

Important Technical Disclosure: This website does not maintain an online database, user registration system, or server-side storage of personal information entered into the booking form.

When you click "Check Availability on WhatsApp" or "Submit on WhatsApp", the website's JavaScript formats your entered text into a prefilled WhatsApp message and opens WhatsApp Web or the WhatsApp mobile application on your device.

The transmission of that message takes place directly between your WhatsApp account and Lee Comfort Kushalnagar's official WhatsApp number (+91 95919 04010). We use the information provided solely to verify room availability, quote total room pricing, and communicate regarding your prospective reservation.
