Hotel Overview

Welcome to Lee Comfort Kushalnagar

Lee Comfort Kushalnagar is a dedicated hotel located in the town of Kushalnagar, Karnataka, situated conveniently on State Highway 88 (SH 88), opposite RMC and right next to Woodland Showroom.

We provide practical, neat, and comfortable accommodation in two main categories: AC Deluxe Rooms and Standard Non-AC Rooms. Our location makes it easy for guests to access local restaurants, shops, public transit, and well-known regional tourist destinations across Coorg.

Sightseeing Guide

Nearby Places & Attractions

Explore Attractions

Lee Comfort Kushalnagar is situated in Kushalnagar town, making it a convenient base for exploring nearby regional attractions. Note: These are independent tourist spots and are not operated or ticketed by the hotel.

Golden Temple (Namdroling)

The renowned Tibetan monastery and Golden Temple in Bylakuppe, situated approximately 5 to 6 km from Kushalnagar town.

Cauvery Nisargadhama

A natural island reserve on the Cauvery River with bamboo groves and deer park, located approximately 3 km from the hotel.

Dubare Elephant Camp

Famous riverbank elephant sanctuary and river rafting point, situated approximately 14 km from Kushalnagar.

Harangi Dam

A scenic masonry dam and reservoir near Kushalnagar, located about 9 km from town.

Kushalnagar Town Centre

Immediate access to local restaurants, shopping outlets, pharmacies, and town bus connectivity on SH 88.

Madikeri & Coorg Hills

A pleasant 30 km drive uphill along SH 88 towards Madikeri fort, Raja's Seat, and Abbey Falls.
